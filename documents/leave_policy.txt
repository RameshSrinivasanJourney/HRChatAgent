1. Purpose
This Leave Policy is designed to establish a structured, transparent, and sustainable leave
framework. The policy aims to balance employee well-being with operational continuity
while standardizing leave accrual, utilization, accumulation, and encashment practices
across the organization.
2. Policy Scope
This policy sets out the various types of leave that an employee is eligible for and outlines
the procedure for availing a leave. Your leaves are calculated based on a calendar year i.e.,
from 1st January to 31st December.
3. Annual Leave Entitlement (Total: 20 Days)
 Casual Leave (CL): 12 days per calendar year
 Earned Leave (EL): 8 days per calendar year
 EL Accumulation Cap: Maximum of 20 days
 Leave Credit Cycle: Leave credits are processed quarterly (5 days per quarter)

Leave Category Quarterly Credit Leave Credit Status
Casual Leave (CL) 3 days Credit beginning of each Quarter
Earned Leave (EL) 2 days Credit at the end of each Quarter
4. Types of Leave and Entitlements
4.1 Casual Leave (CL)
 Employees are entitled to 12 days of Casual Leave (CL) in a calendar year.
 CL is intended for short-term or unforeseen personal requirements.
 CL cannot be carried forward or encashed.
 Unutilized CL will lapse at the end of the calendar year.
4.2 Earned Leave (EL)
 Employees are entitled to 8 days of Earned Leave (EL) per calendar year.
 Earned Leave can be carried forward to the next calendar year.
 EL can be accumulated up to a maximum of 20 days.
 EL becomes eligible for encashment once the balance reaches 20 days.
 No further EL will accrue beyond 20 days unless encashment is availed.
 Earned Leave (EL) entitlement for new joiners will be activated only after
successful completion of the probation period.
 Earned Leave may be used for planned or long-term leave requirements.
5. Leave Utilization Order
Leaves must be utilized in the following order:
1. Casual Leave (CL)
2. Earned Leave (EL)
Any deviation from this order requires management approval.
6. Earned Leave (EL) Encashment Policy
 Earned Leave (EL) encashment is applicable only when the EL balance
reaches 20 days.
 EL encashment may be availed after the completion of the calendar year.
 Employees may encash EL partially or fully, as per their requirement (for
example, if 20 days are encashed, the remaining 10 days will continue in the
EL account).
 Employees may apply for Earned Leave (EL) encashment on a quarterly basis.
 Employees are permitted to take encashment at one time in a calendar year.
 The remaining EL balance will continue to reflect in the employee’s EL
account after encashment.
 Encashment will be calculated based on the Basic component of CTC, and it
will be processed only through payroll, and applicable statutory taxes will be
deducted accordingly.
 Earned Leave (EL) encashment is applicable only to active employees.
 Employees who are serving their notice period or have submitted their
resignation are not eligible for EL encashment, including at the time of exit.
7. Transition of Existing Privilege Leave (PL) to Earned Leave (EL)
7.1 PL to EL Conversion Logic
As part of the policy transition, existing Privilege Leave (PL) balances will be reviewed and
aligned under the new structure. This will be a one-time exercise to convert eligible PL into
Earned Leave (EL) for employees holding PL balances.
Employees who currently have Privilege Leave (PL) may choose either to convert their PL
into Earned Leave (EL) or to continue with their existing PL balance. This conversion is
optional and, if chosen, will be done once on the effective of this policy.
Conversion Logic
Case 1: If PL Balance ≤ 6 Days
 If an employee’s PL balance is less than or equal to 6 days,
→ The PL balance will remain unchanged in the PL account.
 No transfer to EL will occur.
 Case 2: If PL Balance Exceeds 6 Days
 If an employee’s Privilege Leave (PL) balance exceeds 6 days, the following conversion
logic shall apply:
1. Standard Deduction:
Six (6) or seven (7) days will be retained from the existing PL balance, depending on
whether the current balance is an even or odd number.
Eg: 1. Current PL balance is an even number: If your current PL balance in 10, you
will be eligible for 6 days standard deduction.
2. Current PL balance is an odd number: If your current PL balance is 11, you will
be eligible for 7 days standard deduction.
2. Conversion-EarnedLeave(EL):
After the applicable standard deduction, the remaining Privilege Leave (PL) balance
shall be converted into Earned Leave (EL) in the ratio 2:1 and credited to the
employee’s EL account. The standard retained balance of six (6) or seven (7) days will
continue to remain in the PL account and can be used until fully exhausted, with no
expiry.
For Eg: If your PL as on date is 10 days, 6 days standard deduction will be applied,
and you will continue to have those 6 days in PL, and the remaining 4 days will be
converted to 2 days EL.
If your PL as on date is 11 days, 7 days standard deduction will be applied, and you
will continue to have those 7 days in PL, and the remaining 4 days will be converted
to 2 days EL.
7.2 PL to EL Conversion – Calculation Examples
Example 1 (Even Number)
•PL Balance: 10 days
• Deducted from PL: 6 days
• Remaining PL: 4 days
• EL credited = In the ratio 2 PL :1 EL = 2 EL days
Final Outcome:
– PL Balance: 6
– Additional Credit to EL : 2 days
Example 2 (Odd Number)
• PL Balance: 13 days
• Deducted from PL: 7 days
• Remaining PL: 6 days
• EL credited = In the ratio 2 PL :1 EL = 3 EL days
Final Outcome:
– PL Balance: 7
– Additional Credit to EL: 3 days
Example 3 (Less than or equal to 6 days)
• PL Balance: 6 days
• Since PL ≤ 6
• No conversion applied
Final Outcome:
– PL Balance: 6 days
– Additional Credit to EL: Zero (No conversion of EL to PL)
8. Additional Leave Categories
The following leave categories are governed by statutory requirements and the company’s
internal policies. These leaves are over and above the annual leave entitlement and do not
form part of CL or EL balances.
8.1 Maternity Leave
a. In Case of Pregnancy
1. Maternity Leave benefits are governed by Indian labour laws, including the Maternity
Benefit Act, 1961, and applicable amendments.
2. Employees become eligible for Maternity Leave after completing 80 days of
confirmed employment with RGKTech.
3. Eligible employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid Maternity Leave for the first and
second child, as per company records.
4. For the third or subsequent child, employees are entitled to 12 weeks of paid
Maternity Leave.
5. Maternity Leave may be split before and after delivery—up to 8 weeks before the
expected delivery date, with the remaining period after childbirth.
6. A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner stating the expected
date of delivery must be submitted along with the leave request.
7. Employees wishing to extend leave beyond the entitled Maternity Leave period may
apply for unpaid special leave after exhausting their Privilege Leave (PL). The total
period of absence, including Maternity Leave and extended leave, shall not exceed
eight months. Upon return, the employee will be reinstated to the same position
held prior to Maternity Leave.
8. Any extension beyond the statutory Maternity Leave period is subject to
management discretion.
9. Any extension beyond the statutory Maternity Leave period shall first be adjusted
against the employee’s available leave balance. Once all eligible leave balances are
exhausted, the remaining period of absence will be treated as Leave Without Pay
(LWP).
b. In Case of Adoption
1. Maternity Leave provisions for adopting mothers are governed by Indian labour
laws.
2. Maternity Leave for adopting mothers is applicable only if the adopted child is below
three months of age.
3. Eligible adopting mothers are entitled to 12 weeks of paid Maternity Leave,
commencing from the date of adoption.
4. Extension of leave beyond the entitled period may be requested as unpaid special
leave after exhausting PL, subject to the maximum absence period of eight months.
5. All extensions are subject to management approval.
8.2 Paternity Leave
1. Paternity Leave is applicable for the birth or adoption of the first and second child
only, as per company records.
2. Eligible employees are entitled to 5 days of Paternity Leave per delivery or adoption.
3. Leave requests must be supported with valid documentation (medical or adoptionrelated) and approved in advance by the reporting manager.
4. Paternity Leave may be availed:
 Within four weeks before or after the spouse’s delivery, or
 Within four weeks of adoption of a child. Any Paternity Leave not availed
within this period will be considered forfeited.
9. General Leave Rules & Administration
9.1 Leave Application & Approval
All leave requests must be submitted through iAssistant and approved in advance by the
reporting manager.
Path: iAssistant → Leave Request → Apply Leave Request
9.2 Unapproved Absence / Leave Without Pay (LWP)
Any leave or absence not applied for or approved in iAssistant will be treated as Leave
Without Pay (LWP).
Additionally, if an employee neither submits a Leave/Work From Home request in
iAssistant nor has an official access entry recorded for the day, the absence will be
considered LWP.
9.3 Holidays and Weekly Offs During Leave
If a declared public holiday or a weekly off falls within an approved leave period, such
day(s) shall not be counted as leave.

Management reserves the right to amend, modify, suspend, or withdraw any part of this
policy at its discretion, in accordance with applicable statutory requirements and
organizational needs. This policy will be reviewed annually or as required by HR.

Leave Policy – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
1. What is the total number of leaves I am entitled to in a year?
You are entitled to 20 days of paid leave per calendar year, comprising:
 12 days of Casual Leave (CL)
 8 days of Earned Leave (EL)
2. What is the leave year followed by the company?
The leave year follows the calendar year, from 1st January to 31st December.
3. How and when are leaves credited?
Leaves are credited on a quarterly basis as follows:
 Casual Leave (CL): 3 days per quarter (Credit beginning of each quarter)
 Earned Leave (EL): 2 days per quarter (Credit end of each quarter)
Total quarterly credit: 5 days
4. Can I carry forward my unused Casual Leave (CL)?
No. Casual Leave cannot be carried forward or encashed. Any unutilized CL will lapse at
the end of the calendar year.
5. Can Earned Leave (EL) be carried forward?
Yes. Earned Leave can be accumulated up to a maximum of 20 days.
6. What happens if my EL balance reaches 20 days?
Once your EL balance reaches 20 days:
 You become eligible for EL encashment
 EL will not accrue further unless you opt for encashment
7. When can I encash my Earned Leave?
EL encashment can be availed:
 Only after the completion of the calendar year
 Only if your EL balance is 20 days
8. In what slabs can I encash my EL?
There are no fixed slabs for Earned Leave (EL) encashment. Once an employee’s EL
balance reaches 20 days, they become eligible to encash Earned Leave. Employees may
choose the number of days they wish to encash as per their requirement. For example, if
an employee opts to encash 15 days, the remaining 15 days will continue to reflect in the
EL account.
9. How is EL encashment paid?
EL encashment is:
 Processed only through payroll
 Subject to applicable statutory tax deductions
10. In what order should leaves be utilized?
Leaves must be utilized in the following order:
1. Casual Leave (CL) and Privilege Leave (PL)
2. Earned Leave (EL)
Any deviation from this order requires management approval.
11. What happens to my existing Privilege Leave (PL)?
Your existing PL balance will be transitioned as per the PL to EL conversion logic defined in
the policy.
12. What if my PL balance is 6 days or less?
 Your PL balance will remain unchanged
 No conversion to EL will take place
13. What if my PL balance exceeds 6 days?
If your Privilege Leave (PL) balance exceeds six (6) days, six (6) days or seven days (7) will
be deducted from your PL balance as defined in the policy. Remaining PL after standard
deduction will be converted to EL in the ratio 2:1. After the conversion, the PL balance will
stand at six (6)/seven (7) days.
14. How is an odd PL balance handled during conversion?
In case of PL balance being an odd number, seven days (7) will remain in the PL and the
remaining PL will be converted to EL in the ratio 2:1.
15. How do I apply for leave?
All leave requests must be submitted through iAssistant:
 Path: iAssistant → Leave Request → Apply Leave Request
 Leave must be approved by your reporting manager.
16. What happens if I do not apply for leave in iAssistant?
Any absence without an approved leave or WFH request in iAssistant will be treated as
Leave Without Pay (LWP).
17. What will happen to my final Privilege Leave (PL) balance after conversion to
Earned Leave (EL)?
After the conversion, the applicable standard deduction of six (6) days or seven (7) days
will remain in your Privilege Leave (PL) account till you exhaust them.
18. What if I neither apply for leave nor have an access entry?
If there is:
 No leave/WFH request and
 No official access entry recorded
The absence will be treated as LWP.
19. Are holidays and weekly offs counted as leave?
No. If a public holiday or weekly off falls during an approved leave period, it will not be
counted as leave.
20. Am I eligible to encash my Earned Leave (EL) during my notice period or at the time
of exit from the company?
 No. Employees who are serving their notice period or who have submitted their resignation
are not eligible for Earned Leave (EL) encashment, including at the time of exit from the
company.
